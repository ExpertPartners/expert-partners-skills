# Weekly Webinar Playbook

Build the playbook before the skill-driven presentation. Use current business sources for facts and outside references for structure. Do not replace the presenter's direction with an outside formula.

## Read the source packet

Read the current funnel plan, offer decisions, product file, workbook, proof and order form. Read three to five strong webinar references and record what each contributes, what does not fit and what is not being adopted.

Use xavier-offer-builder in source brief mode when the current offer needs extracting. Do not use its construction mode to silently settle missing terms.

Separate approved decisions, presenter direction, suggestions, verified proof and unknown facts. Never treat a confident draft, a reference's results or a file name as proof of business truth.

## Collect the inputs

Collect the audience, title, registration promise, date, time zone, duration, broadcast platform, slide platform, presenter, conductor, agreed offer scope and price, proof sources, actual next-step link, smaller acquisition offer if any, reminder and replay rules, and weekly targets.

Use facts already supplied by the source packet. Mark missing items Not found, and conflicts Conflicting. Ask only a business decision the available sources cannot answer, through the current question route. Continue the independent teaching and rehearsal draft without inventing answers.

A new price does not inherit an old package's deliverables. Resolve scope and price together before writing an audience-ready pitch. Until then, keep the sales section visibly marked as an internal placeholder.

## Build the teaching spine

Identify one central idea and make each teaching point support it. Follow the presenter's documented sequence, language and teaching style. Label proposed titles, timing, exercises and structures as proposals until decided.

Use examples to explain, not to invent proof. Outside experts' testimonials and numbers are not the business's results. Do not invent stories, income claims, guarantees, urgency, scarcity, bonuses or audience targets.

Keep the primary offer, strategy-call route and smaller acquisition offer distinct. A reference's two-path close, launch calendar or expiring bonus is not automatically the business's plan.

## Deliver in this order

1. Purpose and boundary
2. Reference contributions and non-adoptions
3. Known direction
4. Missing inputs and offer conflicts
5. Central idea and teaching sequence
6. Proposed rehearsal sequence
7. Proof and language checks
8. Weekly operating loop
9. Real-task test evidence
10. Source links

The rehearsal sequence covers the opening promise, problem recognition, teaching and questions, consequences, summary, transition to help, agreed offer recap, one real next step and questions. Use timing only when supplied or clearly proposed. Never present an internal placeholder as a working booking or checkout link.

The operating loop records registrations, attendees, show rate, acquisition-offer sales by tested price, calls booked and completed, primary-offer sales, collected revenue and ad spend. Keep cohorts and offer revenues separate. Compute show rate as attendees divided by registrations, and report it as not calculable when registrations are zero. Targets come from current business decisions, never a default benchmark.

## Test on the real draft

Before drafting, save the actual source packet and expected checks. After drafting, compare the same task against those checks. Preserve the input, output and comparison in the owning business brain.

Check the presenter's direction, reference attribution, missing facts, commercial conflicts, proof, offer roles and actual next step. A revised source requires a revised draft and the same comparison again. Fix the draft rather than changing an expected check to make it pass.

Distinguish an AI draft comparison, a marketplace skill invocation, a founder rehearsal, a funnel test and a delivered webinar. Evidence of one never proves the others. If the source requires a person's hands-on practice or teaching, leave that open until observed.

## Save and report

Save the playbook and test evidence as Google Docs in the current owning business output folder. Apply the live formatting and file naming rules. Return exact document titles as clickable links.

Private business content and course sources stay in their owning brain, not this repository. This skill does not build or launch funnel assets, send invitations, buy traffic, publish a presentation or advance a course week.
