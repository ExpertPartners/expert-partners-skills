# DM to Dollars

This skill owns the conversation layer. Content earns attention, the profile converts attention into conversations, conversations convert into sales. rare-content-engine writes what gets published, xavier-offer-builder builds what gets sold, this skill runs everything between the scroll-stop and the sale.

Provenance and boundary. This skill operationalizes Allison Gaddy's Expert Partners application of the DM to Dollars playbook and Don Hobbs's hooks-and-carousels work product. The course worksheets and the lead-flow calculator template are copyrighted library material and are never quoted, pasted, or republished here or in any asset this skill produces. Read the plugin's shared reference at plugins/content-engines/references/titans-core.md before working; it is how this family of skills thinks.

## Engine one, the profile audit

The profile is the storefront the DM walks out of. Audit it before any conversation strategy, because a weak profile silently kills conversations that never start. Score each dimension 1 to 5, and for every score under 4, name the one tweak that moves it.

1. Ideal client fit. Does the profile filter out the wrong people and attract the one dream customer? The niche is stated plainly and the transformation delivered is visible in the first read.
2. Vibe matches price point. The energy, authority, and design quality of the profile must match the price of the offer. A high-ticket offer with a chaotic, inconsistent profile reads as a mismatch, and mismatch reads as risk.
3. Curiosity hook in the bio. The bio's job is one reaction, "tell me more." One curiosity phrase or unique claim, not a resume.
4. Proof in sight. Named, specific results visible without scrolling. Pull from the offer's proof ledger (xavier-offer-builder builds it); a profile shipping without proof is a promise shipping naked.
5. A path. Every profile points somewhere, DM keyword, link, or booking path. No dead ends.

Deliver the audit as a scorecard with one tweak per weak dimension, highest-leverage tweak first.

## Engine two, daily lead-flow math

Cashflow is arithmetic before it is marketing. Four levers, multiplied in order:

daily lead flow x lead quality x conversion rate x lead value = daily cashflow, times roughly thirty for the month.

The working moves:

- Measure the current number on each lever honestly before touching anything. No estimates dressed as data.
- Then ask the one-metric question, if only ONE lever improved, which moves monthly cashflow most? Work that lever only. Scattered effort across all four is how nothing moves.
- Lead quality is a dream-customer question, not a volume question. A smaller flow of dream customers (people who buy and succeed quickly) beats a flood of wrong-fit leads on every downstream number.
- Lead value is a pricing and offer question, raising it usually means the offer got stronger, which is xavier-offer-builder's job. Send it there.

Deliver the math as a small table, current versus target per lever, with the one chosen lever named and the reason it wins.

## Engine three, conversation-to-offer bridging

A DM conversation is a belief chain walked live. The bridge has four moves:

1. Open on their signal. Conversations start from engagement (a comment, a story reply, a DM keyword), never cold spray. Hooks that challenge identity start the most conversations, the pattern from Don's work product, "you are not X, you are just Y" tension that makes the right reader feel seen and the wrong reader scroll past. Write fresh hooks in the brand's voice; never reuse another brand's lines.
2. Qualify against the dream customer. Two or three genuine questions that reveal whether this is the one customer the offer exists for. If they are not, serve them briefly and release them. No convincing the wrong-fit.
3. Meet the objection with its belief chain. Every hesitation in a DM is a vehicle, internal, or external belief from the offer's false-belief inventory. Answer with the new story and its proof, conversationally, one belief at a time. If the offer has no chain for a recurring objection, that is a finding, send it back to xavier-offer-builder.
4. Bridge to the path. The close of a DM conversation is a doorway, a call booked, a resource sent, a next conversation scheduled. Direct, no pressure theater. The offer's reason-to-act is honest urgency only.

## How other skills consume this

- xavier-offer-builder sits upstream, this skill sells conversationally what that skill built, and returns recurring unanswered objections to it as new belief-chain work.
- rare-content-engine and the content lanes sit at the top, their pieces generate the engagement signals engine three opens on.
- The GHL conversations lane executes what this skill designs, message sequences, DM keyword automations, and follow-up cadences built from the bridge.

## Guardrails

- EPIC is nurture and growth only, never recruiting. No recruiting scripts, no downline pitches, no sponsor-bait conversations enter any EPIC surface, ever.
- This skill designs and drafts. Nothing gets sent to a real person outside the org without Allison's explicit approval, drafts stop at the send gate.
- Dream-customer filter over volume. No mass-DM spray, no engagement-farming, no manufactured intimacy.
- Never use an em dash. Use commas, periods, parentheses, or double hyphens. No colons or semicolons in drafted prose.
- Proof over adjectives, honest urgency only, no hype.
- Brand voice comes from the active brand's skill or canon doc, never assumed. If the target brand is ambiguous, ask before drafting.
