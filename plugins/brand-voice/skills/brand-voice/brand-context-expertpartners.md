# Brand Context -- Expert Partners (EPIC)

## Brand Identity

- Brand Name: Expert Partners (community / inner circle: EPIC, Expert Partners Inner Circle)
- Co-Founders: Allison Gaddy and Don Hobbs
- Category: Real Estate Coaching, Mastermind, and Operator Education
- What We Sell: High-level coaching, mastermind community, live events, training programs, and AI-powered tools for elite real estate operators (top-producing agents, team leaders, brokerage builders)
- Differentiator: Industry Architect and Transformation Authority positioning. Operator-led, not guru-led. Built by people running the business at the highest level, not selling theory from the sidelines.

## Mission and Vision

We build the next generation of real estate leaders by giving operators the systems, mindset, and community required to scale beyond personal production into true business ownership. We architect transformation, we don't motivate around it.

## Target Audience

- Top-producing agents (typically 50+ transactions or $20M+ volume annually)
- Team leaders scaling past 10 agents
- Brokerage owners and emerging market center leaders
- Operators ready to invest in proximity, systems, and identity work, not just tactics

## Brand Voice and Tone

- "We" not "I" -- company / team voice, never first-person solo
- Authoritative, operator-grounded, no theory
- Forward-thinking, infrastructure-minded
- Direct, unfiltered, but professional
- Inspiring through proof (case studies, named operators, methodology), not hype

## Visual Identity

- Color System:
  - Black: dominant
  - Brand Blue: primary accent (specific hex stored in design tokens file)
  - White and supporting neutrals for whitespace and contrast
- Typography:
  - PT Serif: editorial and headline serif
  - Overpass: UI and body
  - Poppins: secondary display and supporting copy
- Aesthetic: editorial, premium, infrastructure-grade. Not luxury fluff. Not motivational poster.

## Core Messaging Pillars

- Industry Architect: we build the operating systems other agents pay for later
- Transformation Authority: real change, named case studies, proprietary methodology
- Proof Architecture: named operators, results, methodology, endorsements (this is our strategic gap to close)
- Build Something That Runs Without You: wealth, financial freedom, leverage as the goal
- Operator Community: peer-to-peer at the highest level, not coach-down

## Products and Programs

- EPIC Inner Circle: high-level mastermind community
- Coaching programs: tier-based, operator-aligned
- Live events and mastermind days
- Micro-SaaS in development: DealFlow AI, ListingLaunch AI, BuyerMatch AI (Steve Larsen partnership context)
- Funnels and webinars for client acquisition (Perfect Webinar framework in active use)

## Spelling and Style Rules

- Don: D-O-N (Don Hobbs)
- Allison Gaddy: spelled correctly
- eXp: lowercase "e," uppercase "XP" when referenced
- "We" voice for all brand statements (never "I")
- eXcelerate: not "accelerate" when referencing the brand-related term
- Melony: not Melanie <!-- brand-lint: ignore -->
- No em dashes. Use commas, semicolons, colons, parentheses, or double hyphens.

## Things We Never Say

- "Just believe in yourself"
- "Crush it" or "Go get 'em"
- Hype language with no methodology
- "Great question" or empty validation
- Apologies for being direct
- All-caps emphasis or excessive exclamation points

## Operating Principles

- Identity before strategy
- Proof over performance
- Systems over symptoms
- Operator-led, not guru-led
- Build for transferability: if it can't run without you, you don't own it yet
- Open playbook within the community, premium positioning to the market
