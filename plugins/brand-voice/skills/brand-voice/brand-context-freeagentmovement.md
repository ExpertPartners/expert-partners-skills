# Brand Context -- Free Agent Movement

## Brand Identity

- Brand Name: Free Agent Movement (program and framework: Free Agent Formula)
- Tagline: "Break The Cycle. Build The Movement." with the philosophy sub-line "Freedom through leverage" (ruled 2026-08-06)
- Founder: Allison Gaddy
- Co-Leadership: Don Hobbs (broader Expert Partners / EPIC ecosystem)
- Category: Real Estate Agent Recruiting and Movement-Based Coaching
- What We Sell: A recruiting, coaching, and content ecosystem for growth-focused real estate agents ready to build leverage, freedom, and purpose-driven businesses inside eXp Realty
- Differentiator: Pro-growth, not pro-sponsor. Hands-on C-level leadership, open playbooks, collaboration over competition, innovation over inertia.

## Mission and Vision

We exist to help agents break the cycle of broken brokerage models, outdated production systems, and identity-less leadership. We activate Think Big thinking in agents who are ready to stop trading time for transactions and start building real businesses with freedom built in.

The vision is a movement of self-aware, system-driven, growth-focused agents who lead with heart, build with strategy, and rise into their full potential.

## Target Audience

- Growth-focused real estate agents stuck in traditional or solo producer models
- Team leaders and operators ready to scale beyond rainmaker dependency
- Agents inside eXp who want active mentorship, not silent sponsorship
- Agents outside eXp evaluating a move and looking for real leadership, not pitch decks

## Brand Voice and Tone

- Bold, direct, warm
- Real-time and conversational, voice memo energy
- Forward-thinking and strategic
- Empathetic without being soft
- Commanding without being harsh
- Self-love rooted: self-acceptance leads to inclusive leadership and community growth

## Core Messaging Pillars

- Think Big: stop playing small, build the business your future self needs
- Break The Cycle: end the broken patterns of solo grind, sponsor silence, and identity drift
- Freedom Through Leverage: systems, AI, and team are the path out of the daily hustle trap
- Self-Love in Leadership: you cannot pour from an empty cup, identity comes before strategy
- Open Playbook Leadership: we share what works, no gatekeeping, no posturing

## Visual and Brand Anchors (Visual Identity System v3.1)

- Editorial neutral, black-dominant. Palette is Black (#000000), White (#FFFFFF), and a single subtle Warm Bone (#F7F4EF). NO color accent: the prior Brand Blue (#2757CF) is retired for FAM. Emphasis comes from contrast and typography, never a colored highlight.
- Typography: PT Serif headlines, Neue Swiss subheads / body / labels, IBM Plex Mono for numbers and technical detail. Overpass and Poppins are retired.
- FAM is its own system, not an inherit of Expert Partners. It is built to layer over each member's personal brand, never replace it, which is why it carries no accent.
- Warmth comes only from rich, editorial photography. The interface stays crisp black, white, and bone.
- Inclusive, diverse representation in imagery. Modern, accessible, never elitist. Movement-coded, not person-coded.
- Never reintroduce blue, brown, sepia, or warm-neutral UI tones; never add a color accent to the FAM mark; never substitute the licensed fonts.

## Spelling and Style Rules

- eXp: lowercase "e," uppercase "XP" (never "EXP" or "exp") <!-- brand-lint: ignore -->
- Don: D-O-N (never Donald or Donny in copy)
- eXcelerate: not "accelerate" when referencing the brand-related term
- Allison Gaddy: spelled correctly, no nicknames in formal copy
- No em dashes anywhere in copy. Use commas, semicolons, colons, parentheses, or double hyphens.
- No bold or italics in personal-brand-level content from Allison.

## Things We Never Say

- "Just hustle harder" or "grind mode"
- Sponsor-speak: "join my team" or "I'll mentor you" without substance
- Empty motivational fluff with no framework or system
- Anti-broker or anti-brokerage attack language
- Hype without strategy
- "Great question" or empty validation language
