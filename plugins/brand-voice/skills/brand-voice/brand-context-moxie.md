# Brand Context — Moxie Signature Group

## Brand Identity

- Brand Name: Moxie Signature Group
- Founder and Lead: Allison Gaddy
- Brokerage: eXp Realty (eXp Luxury designation used selectively where applicable, but not the brand's primary positioning lever)
- Category: Residential Real Estate Team
- Market: North Dallas, with concentration in Frisco and Prosper
- Price Point: $500K+, with sweet spot at $700K to $1.2M
- What We Sell: Full-service residential real estate representation (buyer and seller) with an elevated client experience, deep local market expertise, and a digital-first transaction process
- Differentiator: Elevated, accessible premium. Luxury brand feel without luxury alienation. We deliver high-touch service to clients across the upper-mid and lower-luxury segments.

## Mission and Vision

We help discerning North Dallas buyers and sellers move with confidence, clarity, and a level of service that respects their time, their investment, and their next chapter. We build the kind of real estate team people refer their family to.

## Target Audience

- Move-up buyers and sellers in Frisco, Prosper, and surrounding North Dallas suburbs
- Relocation buyers entering the DFW market
- Sellers in the $500K to $1.5M range looking for marketing depth, not just a sign in the yard
- Investors and second-home buyers in the area

## Brand Voice and Tone

- Elevated, but warm and accessible
- Confident, never pretentious
- Clear, professional, client-first
- Inclusive language: we don't talk down to non-luxury buyers, and we don't oversell to luxury buyers
- Modern, digital-fluent, but personal

## Visual Identity

- Typography:
  - Playfair Display: editorial and display serif (signature feel)
  - Montserrat: body and supporting UI (clean, modern, accessible)
- Aesthetic: elevated, accessible, modern, North Dallas market sensibility (not Beverly Hills, not generic suburb)
- Imagery: real homes, real neighborhoods, real clients where possible. AI-generated home exteriors (PixVerse, iStock AI) used carefully for city-specific landing pages.

## Core Messaging Pillars

- Signature Service: every client gets a level of attention that earns referrals
- Local Authority: we know Frisco, Prosper, and North Dallas at street-by-street depth
- Digital-First, High-Touch: virtual operations, digital transactions, real human relationship
- Elevated, Accessible: premium experience without the gatekeeping
- Move With Confidence: clarity at every step of the transaction

## Spelling and Style Rules

- Moxie Signature Group: full name in formal contexts, "Moxie" acceptable in casual or social
- eXp Realty: lowercase "e," uppercase "XP" (when referenced)
- Allison Gaddy: spelled correctly
- Avoid leaning on eXp Luxury as a primary brand identifier (per positioning strategy)
- No em dashes. Use commas, semicolons, colons, parentheses, or double hyphens.

## Things We Never Say

- Aggressive luxury language that excludes mid-range buyers
- Generic "your trusted realtor" filler
- Stock-photo emotional copy with no substance
- Sponsor-speak or recruiting language in client-facing channels (Moxie is the real estate brand, recruiting belongs to Free Agent Movement)
- Promises about market timing or guaranteed outcomes

## Tech Stack and Operational Context

- CRM: Follow Up Boss (21-stage pipeline, 54 emoji-tagged taxonomy)
- Lead source migration: 584 Lofty leads converted into FUB
- Marketing automation: GoHighLevel
- Web analytics: Google Tag Manager and Ahrefs (cleaned and current)
- Imagery generation: PixVerse and iStock AI for city-specific exterior images

- Virtual operations and digital transactions as default

## Operating Principles

- Client first, always
- Signature service is non-negotiable
- Local expertise wins over generic broker positioning
- Premium feel, accessible language
- Refer-worthy at every touchpoint
