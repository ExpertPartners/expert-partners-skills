# Anti AI Writing Guide

This bundled reference is an independently written editorial implementation, not the private recovered course attachment. `brand-guardian` loads it by default. It does not create company policy or replace current voice canon. Including it in a personal voice guide is optional.

## Read in context

Start with the actual audience, facts, speaker and approved voice. Compare the draft to authentic samples and current shared writing rules. Where canon deliberately differs from a generic suggestion here, canon wins.

If exact historical wording is requested, retrieve the private owning source through the Cal Instruction Ledger and Week 2 Preparation Packet. Apply its original-frame corrections and disclose remaining gaps. Do not publish that source text, guess clipped tails or claim this reference is a verbatim edition.

## Editorial checks

- Rhythm. Notice accidental uniform sentence lengths, repeated paragraph openings and excessive isolated one-line paragraphs. Revise for natural connected thought, not a fixed sentence-length formula.
- Substance. Remove filler, generic hype, needless jargon and repeated transitions when they add no meaning. Preserve precise approved terminology even if a generic word list dislikes it.
- Structure. Check repetitive contrast headers, staged revelations, over-explained emotion and ornamental summary endings. Keep a deliberate contrast when authentic voice and meaning warrant it.
- Specificity. Use supported names, dates and numbers when relevant and permitted. A missing fact stays missing. Never invent proof to make prose feel human.
- Questions and next steps. Distinguish engagement bait from a real question or a useful authorized next step. Do not erase a genuine calibrated invitation.
- Endings. Prefer the selected speaker's supported closing behavior. Never paste a saved example email, signature close or example deadline into unrelated work.
- Presentation. Follow current shared punctuation and formatting rules. Do not replace them with a historical guide's exceptions.
- Accuracy and privacy. Preserve attribution and context. Do not infer finances, family structure, identity or results, and do not expose internal information.

## Final pass

Score the reviewed draft from 0 to 100 against this guide. Return the score, `PASS` at 90 or above or `FAIL` below 90, and the problems together. Explain the score from the draft, without inventing a weighted formula. A passing score never removes problems or overrides current company canon, accepted voice calibration or unresolved factual and brand findings. An audit reports the result; a requested revision improves failing copy and scores the revision again before delivery.

Read the writing aloud, or inspect cadence when audio is unavailable. Check whether the revision preserves meaning and sounds like the selected speaker. Keep clean copy separate from editorial notes.

Do not enforce a universal word ban, fixed 20 percent cut, rule-of-three quota or a claim that AI detection can prove authorship. A style observation is not a fact-check, source permission or publishing authorization.
