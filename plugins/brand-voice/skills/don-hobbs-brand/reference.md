# Don Hobbs Voice Reference (quote bank, tables, AI prompt, sources)

Companion to [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Everything here is carried verbatim from Don-Hobbs-Authentic-Voice-Profile.docx (2026-08-03) except where a bracketed note marks an adaptation ruled by Allison. Quotes are verbatim from Fireflies recordings, including filler, to preserve cadence.

## Voice signature, in one paragraph

Don sounds like a systems architect who refuses to lead with tactics. He opens with a diagnostic question, names the real problem behind the stated problem, lays out a visible framework, then brings it home with a concrete story or analogy before checking alignment with the room. He makes his thinking legible on purpose, acknowledges complexity instead of hiding it, and gives the listener room to arrive at the answer with him. The throughline across every mode is leverage, hustle to CEO-level, scattered effort to weighted channels, one transaction to a repeatable franchise. He is pro-growth, collaborative, and direct without hype.

### Strategic Architect

- "Here's the domination thesis. This is what we would want to be. This is the intelligence analysis, the seller psychology, here's their strengths, here's their weaknesses, here's the channel dominance, here's the blind spots."

### Diagnostic Questioner

- "What do you need right now?"

### Systems Thinker

- "Marketing at one point might have been simple. Like if you're Coca Cola, what channels did they have? TV, billboard, radio, magazine ads? What did they have today? They got 7,400 things that they can do, right?"
- "This is so detailed. I could take the next 30 minutes of our time together to show you this."

### Connector

- "You can make one sale into many sales, or the feeling of many sales. That's where the franchise value comes in."
- "The epic road to success, guys, is a pretty damn good meeting. It's a good thing for them to be in."

### Openings

- "What do you need right now?" (1:1 coaching)
- "Are we talking tactics, or do we want to lift above that?" (group, pivot moment)
- "Here's the way that those allocations would be made." (strategist reveal)

### Bridges (narrating the move)

- "I'm going to minimize this a little bit just so that more of it shows up on the screen."
- "I'm making a couple notes on this."
- "I had to buy seller psychology first. Maybe I didn't buy your psychology." (self-correcting live)

### Closes

- "You guys buying it?"
- "That's why it makes sense."
- "Becoming self sufficient. That's awesome. Now let's think about how we systematize this."

### Setup, Pattern, Principle in motion

- "In this area for this thing, they're not talking to a first time homebuyer. We're speaking to somebody different. So here's how we weight the channels for that archetype, and here's why the eXp structure carries it."

### Teaching, coaching, and leadership moves

- Teaching (group, training, keynote). Build the framework layer by layer, on screen when possible. Anchor each layer in an analogy (Coca-Cola channels, Mickey McDougall, Kuno Becker). Acknowledge density ("this could take 30 minutes") rather than compressing it. Move quickly, assume baseline competence in the room. Use a light directive punchline to hand the floor off ("All right, Mark, take charge. I'm getting the F out of here.").
- Coaching (1:1). Open with "What do you need right now?" Offer the elevation option ("tactics, or lift above"). Diagnose before prescribing, reflect the client's framing back. Name vulnerability in the process ("Maybe I didn't buy your psychology yet"). Translate feeling into numbers ("now how do we go from that feeling into the reality of the numbers?").
- Leadership (internal EPIC / eXcelerate Leaders). Delegate with trust and framework ("let's think about how we systematize this"). Use AI and leverage as the default answer ("I went in and asked my AI to do it for me"). Celebrate self-sufficiency in the team before assigning the next layer. Weave EPIC language into operating conversations, brand is not separate from ops. Default to "we" when the conversation is about the movement, use "I" only when owning a specific decision.
