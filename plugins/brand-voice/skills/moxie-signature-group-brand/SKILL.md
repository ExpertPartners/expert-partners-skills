# Moxie Signature Group Brand System

> **BENCHED 2026-07-30.** Allison tabled the Moxie Signature Group brand (aura ADR 0045). The `moxie` repo is archived read-only. The consumer-facing real estate lane is now **AG Luxury** (`ag-luxury`). This skill is retained for revival only; do not apply it to new work.

This skill applies Moxie Signature Group brand standards. Moxie is Allison Gaddy's North Dallas real estate team operating under eXp Realty.

## Brand positioning

**Tagline north star**: Elevated Accessible Premium

Moxie serves the $700K to $1.2M segment in North Dallas, primarily Frisco and Prosper. The brand promise is luxury-level service for real families. Not stuffy. Not exclusionary. Not the "millionaire club" aesthetic. Polished, warm, inclusive, intentional.

Think: high-end concierge meets approachable family advisor. Premium without pretension.

## The non-negotiables (apply every time)

### Naming and spelling

- Moxie or Moxie Signature Group (both acceptable; Moxie alone for casual context, full name for formal/legal/listing).
- eXp Realty (lowercase "e," uppercase "XP"). Never "EXP," never "Exp."
- Allison Gaddy. Never "Alison" or "Allyson."
- Frisco, Prosper (Texas towns; capitalize).

### eXp Realty co-branding rules

- Include eXp Realty branding ONLY when TREC (Texas Real Estate Commission) requires it. This means: official listings, listing presentations, advertising materials that legally must show brokerage affiliation, contracts.
- For brand identity, social presence, and marketing where eXp identification is not legally required: lead with Moxie.
- Never reference eXp Luxury. Moxie operates outside that brand line.

### Visual identity

- Primary brand icon: geometric endless knot. Not keys. Not house silhouettes. Not generic real estate iconography.
- Typography: Playfair Display headlines, Montserrat body. No exceptions.
- Color, layout, spacing: see `references/tokens.md`.

### Formatting (carries over from Allison's standard rules)

- Never use em dashes. Substitutes: comma, semicolon, colon, parentheses, double hyphen if needed.
- No bold or italics in visible output unless required for legal/regulatory compliance (e.g., MLS).
- No page breaks, horizontal rules, visual dividers.
- Plain text headers and paragraph breaks only.

## Voice (Moxie-specific, distinct from FAM/EPIC)

### Core voice attributes

- Polished but warm. Confident but not boastful.
- Speaks to real families about real homes that happen to be at a premium price point.
- Inclusive language. Never positions luxury as exclusive or aspirational-out-of-reach.
- Strategic and informed. Treats clients as intelligent decision-makers.
- Quietly confident, not performatively luxe.

### How Moxie copy differs from EPIC/FAM copy

- Less "voice memo," more "trusted advisor."
- Slower cadence. Fuller sentences. More descriptive language for listings and lifestyle copy.
- Still no fluff. Still no banned phrases. Still no em dashes.
- Where EPIC is coaching energy, Moxie is hospitality energy.

### Phrases that fit Moxie

- "Thoughtfully designed for"
- "Curated for the way you actually live"
- "Built around your priorities, not the market's"
- "Real homes for real families"
- "Premium service. No pretense."
- "Where elevated meets accessible"

### Phrases to avoid

- "Luxury living redefined" (cliche)
- "Your dream home awaits" (cliche)
- "Once in a lifetime" (cliche)
- Anything that sounds like a 1990s glossy real estate ad
- Anything that positions clients as outside the world they are buying into
- Banned phrases inherited from Allison's voice rules (Amazing, Incredible, etc.)

## Listing copy structure

For property listings, follow this pattern:

1. **Lead with the lifestyle or defining feature** (1-2 sentences). What makes this home worth a closer look?
2. **Walk through the home in story order** (paragraph). Entry, primary living, kitchen, primary suite, secondary spaces, outdoor.
3. **Name the elevated details** (paragraph). Finishes, fixtures, custom work, smart home, energy systems.
4. **Anchor in the neighborhood** (paragraph). Schools, walkability, community amenities, proximity to what matters.
5. **Close with an invitation** (1-2 sentences). Direct, warm, low-pressure CTA.

## Output formats

- Listing descriptions: format for MLS character limits and aesthetic flow.
- Buyer/seller guides: PDF or Word document, Moxie branded.
- Social: Instagram-first, then cross-post. Carousel format for listings.
- Email: clean templates, Moxie branded, single CTA per email.
- Presentations: Playfair Display + Montserrat throughout. Endless knot icon present.
