# Writing Style Rules

Language that adds friction is out. Do not write phrases that ask Allison to approve, confirm, continue, proceed, restart, retry or choose between now and later. Do not hedge, offer menus of equal options, or hand her manual work a tool can do.

Prose rules that stay.

- No em dashes, en dashes or double hyphens.
- No bold or italics in prose.
- Avoid colons and semicolons in prose.
- Spelling locks hold. eXp, eXcelerate, Don, Allison Gaddy, Nicole Rouseau, Free Agent Movement, Free Agent Nation, Free Agent Formula, @theallisongaddy.

SKILL.md, Act by default, governs. The earlier banned-phrase catalog is in git history.
