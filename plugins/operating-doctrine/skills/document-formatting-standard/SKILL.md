## Google Docs and Word

Use Poppins throughout, with 1.5 line spacing in every paragraph. Hierarchy comes from size and color, never bold or italics. Apply actual native named paragraph styles so the document outline works. A visually enlarged paragraph is not a heading.

| Role | Native style | Size | Color |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Title | Title | 22 pt | Black #000000 |
| Section | Heading 1 | 17 pt | Black #000000 |
| Subsection | Heading 2 | 13 pt | Brand Blue #2757CF |
| Body | Normal text | 11 pt | Black #000000 |
| Meta or provenance line | Subtitle | 9 pt | Gray #555555 |

Title and Heading 1 retain their existing black color and sizes. The October 18 re-approval does not change them. All named styles use Poppins, 1.5 line spacing, no bold, and no italics.

Preserve the spacing rhythm. Heading 1 has 16 pt before and 8 pt after. Heading 2 has 14 pt before and 6 pt after. Body has 8 pt after, labels 3 pt after, and Title 4 pt after. A subtitle under the title has 14 pt after. Quoted or message-style blocks use body text with a 0.25 inch left indent, without borders or boxes.

Use US Letter, 8.5 by 11 inches, with 1 inch margins on all sides. Use gray Subtitle styling for date, audience, and draft-status lines beneath the title. Do not add horizontal rules, decorative dividers, lines, or emoji.

In Google Docs, set native paragraph styles, then explicitly set Poppins, size, color, bold false, italic false, and paragraph lineSpacing 150. In Word, define the corresponding named styles and apply them to the paragraphs. For docx-js, 1.5 spacing is line 360, and font sizes are in half-points. Title is size 44, Heading 1 is 34, Heading 2 is 26, body is 22, and meta is 18. Name Poppins explicitly in every text run and verify that it renders in the saved output.

## Google Sheets and Excel

Excel follows the same rules as Sheets. Ranger's approved model is [V1 - Sales - Expired Listings 2026 (800k+)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1FYUiGC106jrxvimCBmb2EDiA-RT1yEKN_uekE-2iK24/edit?ouid=103031882690423318011), September 2026 and October 2026. Read it only. The current Decision Log governs if its existing appearance differs.

- Use Poppins 11 in every cell, including the header. Nothing is bold or italic.
- Fill row 1 light gray #EFEFEF. Center every header, use sentence case, and keep it unbolded.
- Freeze row 1 only. Freeze no columns.
- Turn wrapping on and vertically center every cell.
- Set horizontal alignment explicitly in every cell. Center numbers, counts, dates, IDs, codes, and yes or no. Left-align text, names, links, and notes. Never right-align. Row 1 uses its centered-header rule.
- Size columns to their content. Keep the long notes column wide enough for wrapped entries to occupy two or three lines, and check for clipping.
- Use `$#,##0` for currency without cents and `#,##0` for counts. Use a consistent fixed number of decimal places for each decimal measure. Store number-looking IDs as text so leading zeros survive.
- Put clickable links on readable file-name text. Use `None` for an empty value in a populated record. Do not fill unused worksheet space with records or placeholders.
- Keep a tracking workbook as one permanent file with one tab per month. Name tabs like `October 2026` and put them in chronological date order. Do not create a new workbook each month. Keep records in the intended sort order.

## Punctuation and writing

Never use an em dash or a double hyphen in authored prose, including titles, headings, subtitles, labels, captions, messages, and deliverables. Use periods, commas, or parentheses. Colons and semicolons remain allowed sparingly. Preserve required technical notation such as file paths, URLs, configuration syntax, and code without turning it into prose punctuation.
