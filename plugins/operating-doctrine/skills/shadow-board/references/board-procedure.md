# Shadow Board procedure

The working forms for the Shadow Board skill. They restate the steps in SKILL.md and add nothing to them. Where this file and SKILL.md differ, SKILL.md wins.

## The board card

Shown to the user before the board meets, one block per member.

```
Seat <1 to 5>, <full name>
Why they belong. <one or two sentences>
Lens. <the perspective they bring>
Will challenge. <what they will push on>
Declared bias. <the bias they bring to the table>
```

For a one-run swap, show the stand-in's card with the seat's lens unchanged, add one line naming the real published work it is drawn from, and wait for the user's yes.

## The member brief

Every member gets the same brief. Only the persona block changes.

```
Persona
Name. <full name>
Lens. <lens>
Declared bias. <bias>

Question
<the same question for every member>

Business context
<the full business context, word for word, never summarized>

The work
<the work under review, word for word>

Reply with the form below. Every score must cite the exact words in the work that earned it.
```

Launch all five briefs together as separate subagents. When subagents are not available, run them one at a time, give no member another's answer, and note in the review that the members ran one at a time.

## The reply form

```
Score. <0 to 100>
Cited words. "<the exact words in the work that earned the score>"
What works. <...>
What is weak. <...>
What would make it 100. <...>
Verdict. <one or two sentences>
Key points. <1> <2> <3>
Watch-out. <one>
```

Bands. 90 to 100 ready. 75 to 89 solid but needs fixes. 60 to 74 uneven. Under 60 rework the core. No part weights.

## The peer review

One round. Shuffle the five first answers, strip every name and seat, and label them only as Answer A to Answer E. Give each member all five and ask for three things.

```
Agree. <what you agree with, by answer letter>
Push back. <what you push back on, by answer letter>
Shifted. <yes or no, and how your view moved>
```

## The synthesis

One synthesis after the peer review.

```
Core tension. <...>
Differences that remain. <kept, not smoothed over>
Recommendation. <...>
Stop or reject if. <the conditions>
Signs it is working. <observable signs>
```

## Revise and rescore

1. Write one revised version aimed at 100 from every member.
2. Send it to all five members with the same brief and reply form.
3. Repeat at most three times in total. The peer review and the synthesis do not count.
4. After the third pass, or when every member scores 100, stop. If any member is below 100, say plainly what is holding the score down.
5. Show the true scores. List missing inputs the user must supply apart from weak points the work still has, and ask the user for the missing inputs.

## The Doc layout

The Google Doc, in this order.

1. Before and after scores, and the biggest change. On a rerun, the earlier version's score sits beside the new one.
2. The revised work.
3. One tab or section per member, holding that member's card, replies and rescores.
4. The debate, the peer review and then the synthesis.
5. Stop conditions.
6. Success signs.
