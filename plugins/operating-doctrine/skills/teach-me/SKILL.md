# Teach Me, v1.0 (2026-10-25)

HQ's skill for every room that runs a step of Cal's course. It makes each step open the same way and turns each finished step into teaching for anyone who joins Expert Partners, ending with a short AI video. It belongs to HQ and is not one of Cal's skills.

Rulings behind it, all in the live Decision Log. Every step starts in teach-me mode and nothing gets skipped. The skill is called Teach Me and every step ends with a short AI video. Every Expert Partners step gets taught to Don. Start Here is for anyone who joins Expert Partners. How Expert Partners is set up in Claude is called Our AI Org Chart. Read each entry live before relying on it, and never copy its text here.

## Check first

1. Read the live HQ Rulebook, How we build and Document formatting, and the Decision Log entries above.
2. Confirm the step is the next one in Cal's order, week by week. Keeping Cal's order comes first, so anything else that comes up mid-step is written down with its owner and picked up after the step.
3. Read the Library Catalog tab of the Cal Instruction Ledger for the step. Before calling anything missing, check the Ledger and its Gap Build List too.
4. If a read fails, say which one and mark that line unverified. Never fill it from memory.

When Allison says Teach me in the middle of other work, and not at the edge of a course step, follow the Teach me mode paragraph in the HQ Rulebook instead of the two moments below.

## Order and skips

Every step Cal teaches runs in order and none is skipped, including steps already logged. Whatever a step needs that we do not have is made as our own and labeled Rebuilt, handouts included. Only Allison can skip a step, and her ruling is in the Decision Log, so the brief says so when it applies.

## Moment 1, before a step

Open with this line, exactly.

Next step, Teach Me

Then a CEO brief for someone who has never watched Cal's videos or attended the training, under 200 words in total, with these seven parts in this order. Each part is a short plain label on its own line, then its text.

1. What we're doing, one or two sentences.
2. Why it matters to the business.
3. Where Cal teaches it. Give the link and the time from the Library Catalog's Where it's taught column, credited to whoever taught it (Cal is she). Make the link text the name of the video or file, and turn a seconds value in the link into a clock time by dividing out the hours and minutes, so 3725 seconds reads 1:02:05. Check the arithmetic twice. When the column is empty, say so, show what Cal teaches nearby, and never invent a source.
4. What we'll make, a skill, document or handout.
5. What done looks like.
6. Your part, what Allison does. Say so plainly when it is nothing.
7. What the HQ guide will show, one line.

Mark anything Cal does not support as HQ's addition. When Cal is silent or her material is missing, say so plainly and bring the gap to Allison as one decision, never filling it with a design of our own. Never paste course text.

## Moment 2, when a step is done

Do not use the Next step, Teach Me line here, it belongs to the moment before a step. Output the drafts for the room to send on, the guide entry, the SOP story when one was written, and the scene list.

### The HQ guide entry

Four fixed parts, in this order, in plain words for anyone who joins.

What it is
Why we have it
What you do with it
Where it lives

End with a source line and the date checked. Where it lives uses friendly names, never system codes.

### The SOP story

Only when an SOP was written in the step. Open with We wrote this when we got to the moment, because what happened. Then Use this when, the steps, and Who to ask.

### The video scene list

A 60 to 90 second explainer as five to seven scenes. Each scene gives its number, its seconds, its on-screen words (short and plain) and what moves. Follow the same four parts as the guide entry and make the last scene Stuck, ask Allison. Close with the source it was made from and the date checked. The film uses Expert Partners typefaces only, PT Serif and Poppins, never Manrope.

## What it never does

It drafts. It does not edit the Lovable app, Notion or Drive, and it does not render video. Where the room has Claude's Motion tool, the room makes the film from the scene list. The room passes the other drafts to the current HQ guide owner, found through job_status, under the current HQ Rulebook section Handoffs between AIs.

Anything bound for the HQ guide covers Expert Partners only, never AG Luxury, and is written for anyone who joins, so it does not name Don unless the room says otherwise.

## Writing rules

Plain spoken words. No em dashes, no colons or semicolons in prose, no bold, no italics. Friendly file names, never system codes or row numbers. Use Cal's terms, Business Brain, Workspace, AI employee and AI Org Chart. Every link is a clickable name, never a raw URL.

## Before sending

1. The opening line is exact and the brief has seven parts, in order, under 200 words.
2. Every taught source is credited, and anything Cal does not support is marked HQ's addition.
3. Each draft has its source and date checked.
4. No AG Luxury material sits in anything bound for the HQ guide.
