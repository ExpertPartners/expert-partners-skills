# Permission request drafts

The skill may draft a request so Allison can approve it in one step. It never sends, schedules or queues one, and it never contacts a photographer, HOA, developer, builder, city or tourism bureau on its own.

## Voice

Write the draft in the requesting brand's voice, read live from that brand's owning canon before drafting, including its public-naming rule. This file carries no voice rules. If the brand is ambiguous, ask which brand before drafting. An internal workspace name never appears in outward requests when the brand's canon says it is internal only (AG Luxury canon names Allison Gaddy as the public brand).

When the consuming application already holds a request template, reuse it as the base and make only the changes canon or the specific request requires.

## What every request must make clear

- Who is asking and for which brand, and the exact image or images (source URL, creator, filename).
- The exact intended uses (website page, social, print, advertising), the brands covered, and whether crops, overlays or heading text are planned.
- Duration requested (perpetual preferred) and whether exclusivity is asked (normally not).
- The credit line offered, and whether page approval or revocation rights are acceptable.
- For a photographer: whether they own the copyright, and whether releases exist for people or property shown.
- For a developer, HOA or builder: who shot the image and whether they hold rights to sublicense it.
- For a city or tourism bureau: whether commercial real estate marketing is within their media terms.
- For a ShareAlike creator: a request for a non-SA license for the named uses.

## After a reply

Record the grant as a `gmail:` pointer and the scope, credit terms and expiry in the record fields. Never paste the correspondence body into the register. Any condition attached to the grant (credit, approval, revocation) goes into `permission_scope.notes`.
