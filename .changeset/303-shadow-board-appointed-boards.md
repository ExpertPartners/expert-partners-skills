Update Shadow Board to v1.2. Keep one skill with two ways to seat the board. By default the skill picks a fresh board and keeps it for the project. A room may instead appoint its own named board, such as a Marketing Board of Directors, naming up to five seats while the skill fills open seats with counterweight lenses and flags a board drawn from one school. The appointed board is saved as a Google Doc in the room's department brain and seated every run unless a fresh board is asked for once. The rest of the review procedure is unchanged.
