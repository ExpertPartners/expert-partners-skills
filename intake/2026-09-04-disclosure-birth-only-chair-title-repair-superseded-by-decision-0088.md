rather than adopting it. Chair title is exactly the TABLE NAME, worker title is

## Why the birth-only limit was retired

Live testing on 2026-09-04 in NORTH'S ESTATE CONTROL TOWER established that
vendor-generated conversation titles drift from the Table name after birth,
while the Table identity must remain stable and human-locatable. The title is
non-authoritative, so a mismatch never changes identity, but it is the
navigation mirror Allison uses to find the chair, so it is maintained whenever
the surface permits or Allison can repair it. Allison then issued the exact
command Lock that, which made this durable truth under decisions 0060 and 0061.
